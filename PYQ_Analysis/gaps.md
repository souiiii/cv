## Boundaries for the next phase

- Start with the taught theory and worked procedures. G1/G2/G8 are the first **additional numerical** routes; G3 completes recurring theory. The other entries should remain short and sit beside their related taught topic when integrated notes are later requested.
- **Scatter is CORE, not an outside-topic gap:** Sir p.13 already provides forward/backscatter paths; class wording says “front scatter.” Make that distinction explicit at diagram level. Video analysis is likewise taught despite no supplied PYQ. Neither absence of recurrence warrants exclusion nor warrants adding external material.
- **Do not restore excluded legacy material:** adjacency/path lengths and pixel-distance axioms, DFT matrix proofs, Hadamard construction and homomorphic filtering remain outside this safety package. A shared word such as “neighbourhood,” “frequency” or “reflectance” is insufficient support. Do not add official-syllabus corner detection, camera calibration or stereo solely for completeness.
- **Data caution:** IPCV Q3(b)'s PDF confirms the frequency table but says “is given as,” not the Markdown's “is rising.” A Q2 and B Q3(a) reuse one input/formula; output clipping is not specified. These are source-use cautions, not extra topics. Details are retained in [question mapping](question_mapping.md).

Stop at these nine bounded extensions. No new study notes, outside research or full textbook treatments are part of this phase.
