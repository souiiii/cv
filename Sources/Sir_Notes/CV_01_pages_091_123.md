## PDF page 92

Noise Models
• Most types of noise are modeled as known probability density functions.
• Noise model is decided based on understanding of the physics of the sources of noise.
  • Gaussian: poor illumination
  • Rayleigh: range image
  • Gamma, exp: laser imaging
  • Impulse: faulty switch during imaging
  • Uniform is least used
• Parameters can be estimated based on histogram on small flat area of an image.

## PDF page 96

Digital Video
• It is a continuous arrangement of image frames that represents movement of object(s), background or movement of both over time.
• A digital video is fundamentally a 3D signal if we consider its two spatial dimensions (x, y), and time (t).
• Important distinction: A video is often called a sequence of 2D signals/images, but mathematically, when the temporal dimension is included, it is a 3D spatiotemporal signal.

## PDF page 100

Video Processing vs Video Analytics

| Aspect | Video Processing | Video Analytics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purpose | Improve, transform, or manipulate video | Extract meaningful information and make decisions |
| Focus | Pixels and frames | Objects, events, and behavior |
| Typical input | Raw video | Raw or processed video |
| Typical operations | Filtering, enhancement, compression, frame extraction, stabilization | Object detection, tracking, classification, activity recognition |
| Output | Enhanced/modified video or processed frames | Information, alerts, events, statistics, decisions |
| AI requirement | Not necessarily | Frequently uses AI/ML/DL |
| Example | Remove noise from a video | Detect a person entering a restricted area |
| Another example | Resize a 4K video to HD | Count vehicles on a road |
| Main question | “How can I process/modify this video?” | “What is happening in this video?” |

## PDF page 111

Gaussian Mixture Model for Background subtraction
• Model a particular pixel as a mixture of Gaussians to represent multiple backgrounds.
• Multiple adaptive Gaussians are necessary to cope with non-static background, illumination variation, underwater vegetation, etc.
• Pixel values that do not fit the background distributions are considered foreground.

## PDF page 112

Wronskian Change Detection Model
Wronskian change detector is a vector image model to detect changes from one frame to another.
Spatio-contextual property of a pixel can be incorporated by including the region of support.

| Acronym | Full form | Main purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FPN | Feature Pyramid Network | Combines high-level semantic features with lower-level spatial features to detect objects at different scales. |
| PAN | Path Aggregation Network | Enhances FPN by adding a bottom-up path, improving flow of localization and low-level information. |
