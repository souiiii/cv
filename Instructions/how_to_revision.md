# How to Build Revision Notes

Build `Notes/revision_notes.md` only after the integrated `Notes/notes.md` and filtered PYQ analysis are complete.

The revision layer must contain **no new scope** and must not become a second full set of notes.

Prioritize:

- formulas and numerical procedures that must be reproduced accurately;
- high-probability theory distinctions and definitions;
- diagrams/flows worth remembering;
- recurring aligned PYQ patterns;
- explicitly mentioned class-guidance points;
- a very small `SUPPLEMENTARY` safety section only if retained by the final analysis.

Because the main notes already integrate theory and worked numericals topic by topic, revision notes should compress both together: a short reminder of the concept followed by the essential formula/procedure or numerical pattern where applicable.

Keep it short enough for a final pre-exam pass. Do not duplicate the full prose explanations or full worked solutions from `Notes/notes.md`.
