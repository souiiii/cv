# How to Build Revision Notes

Build `Notes/revision_notes.md` only after the main notes, numericals and filtered PYQ analysis are complete.

The revision layer must contain no new scope.

Prioritize:

- formulas and numerical procedures that must be reproduced accurately;
- high-probability theory distinctions and definitions;
- diagrams/flows that are worth remembering;
- recurring aligned PYQ patterns;
- explicitly mentioned class-guidance points;
- a very small `SUPPLEMENTARY` safety section only if retained by the final analysis.

Keep it short enough for a final pre-exam pass. Do not duplicate full explanations from `Notes/notes.md` or full worked solutions from `Notes/numericals.md`.
